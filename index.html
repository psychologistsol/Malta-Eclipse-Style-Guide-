SOL Y LUNA

Bachelorx
Malta Eclipse

Six looks, one week, one story. Sol and Luna run through the whole trip, so every day pulls a different piece of that world. These are starting points, not rules. Mix across the lists, borrow, improvise.

82 to 86°F Daytime highs, late September into October
68 to 72°F Evenings, with a real sea breeze after dark
Light Linen, chiffon, satin, lightweight metallic mesh

Sunglasses, all week. Bring two pairs. Dark tints for daytime, beach clubs, and the boat. Light or mirrored tints for sunsets, evenings, and rave lights, where dark lenses just kill the whole look. Heavy fabrics will punish you by 3PM, so skip them entirely.

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30

Valletta Golden Hour

Fond Għadir bathing pools in the morning, Rampila dinner at 4PM, Upper Barrakka Gardens and Valletta after

For her

  • Olive, terracotta, or deep wine slip dress or linen set
  • Warm metals, layered however you like
  • Sandals you can walk Valletta's hills in, the streets are stone and steep
  • Swimsuit and a cover up packed for the morning pools

For him

  • Olive or sand linen shirt, rolled sleeves, trousers or tailored shorts
  • Loafers or clean leather sandals
  • One good piece of jewelry, your call
  • Trunks and a towel for the morning

No white, no cream this day. Not for anyone. Trust us on this one.

Rampila is a place Jay and Carla love. It is one of a kind, dinner under the old city walls, so dress like you mean it.

THURSDAY, OCTOBER 1

Luna Shines Bright

Defected pre-party at Café del Mar, then family afters at Club Barbossa

For her

  • Silver metallic dress or a two piece set
  • Star hair clip, moonstone or crystal drops
  • Silver heels or sandals, whichever survives a long night
  • Silver liner or star face gems

For him

  • Metallic silver or silver toned button up, worn open or closed
  • Black trousers or jeans
  • Silver chain, star pin, or a silver ring
  • White sneakers or loafers

This is the first coordinated night of the week, so it sets the tone for every photo after it. Go fuller than feels necessary.

FRIDAY, OCTOBER 2

Wear What You Want

Glitterbox, Treehaus, Roots, the marathon day across three stages

No theme, on purpose

  • This is the longest day of the festival, so comfort wins
  • Shoes you can stand in for eight hours, not the ones that look best
  • Save the coordinated looks for the other days

A light layer for the walk home at 4AM is the one thing people always forget.

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 3

Disco Sol

Café del Mar, St Paul's Bay, daytime into a 9PM venue transfer

For her

  • Gold or orange sequin, holographic, or metallic top with a slip skirt or wide leg pants
  • Sun shaped sunnies, layered gold jewelry
  • Glitter sun stickers or face gems
  • Bronze or copper shimmer on shoulders and collarbones

For him

  • Bold print or metallic swim trunks
  • Open disco or Hawaiian shirt worn unbuttoned
  • Oversized sunglasses, gold chains, bandana
  • Sandals or slides, you will be near water all day

Loud, sparkly, daytime disco energy. Anything that catches sun is doing its job.

SUNDAY, OCTOBER 4

Total Eclipse

The Beach at Little Armier, Mellïeħa, closing day

The idea

  • Everyone in a black base, then split the accents. Half the group goes gold for Sol, half goes silver for Luna
  • Pick your side when you pack, or decide as a couple and split between you
  • Together the group reads as the eclipse itself, which is the whole reason this trip is named what it is

For her

  • Black dress, set, or swim base
  • All gold or all silver accessories, no mixing within one person
  • Metallic liner or gems in your chosen metal
  • Black sandals or boots

For him

  • Black shirt, trunks, or trousers
  • Chains, rings, and sunglasses frames in your chosen metal
  • Black sneakers or slides

This is the finale and the best group photo of the week, so coordinate your side with whoever you are traveling with before you fly.

TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6

Studio 54 at Sea

Lagoon 400 catamaran from Xemxija, 11AM to sunset, with Lara shooting aboard

For her

  • Metallic or high shine swimsuit with a sheer kaftan or sarong over it
  • Big disco sunglasses, statement earrings, whatever sparkles
  • Wide brim hat for the middle of the day
  • Shimmer body oil, this is the day it photographs best

For him

  • Metallic or bold print trunks with an open camp collar shirt
  • Tinted or mirrored sunglasses, chains, a good hat
  • Barefoot on deck, so bring something easy to slip on at the jetty

Lara is shooting the group on the water, so this is the day the whole week gets documented. No cover ups you would hide behind.

ROOM NIGHT, PALAZZO VIOLETTA

Celestial Nightcap

One night in, everyone together, no venue and no schedule

For her

  • Silky pajama set or slip nightgown in navy, silver, or lilac, star and moon prints welcome
  • Matching robe if you want the coordinated photo
  • Glow bracelets, celestial temporary tattoos

For him

  • Silky pajama set, or a robe over shorts in the same palette
  • Glow bracelet, celestial temp tattoo

Lowest effort night of the week and somehow always the best photos. Pack the robe.
